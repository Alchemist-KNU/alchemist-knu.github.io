Research Themes

응용지구화학연구실의 연구 분야

Environmental Geochemistry

Understanding the fate and transport of trace elements in environmental

  • Trace element behavior in soil and groundwater
  • Identification of natural and anthropogenic sources
  • Geochemical processes controlling element mobility

Keywords

Soil Groundwater Geochemistry Trace Elements

Isotope Geochemistry

Investigating the geochemical behavior of radionuclides for environmental safety and waste disposal

  • Geochemical behaviors of radionuclides
  • Sorption, transport, and speciation
  • Applications to radioactive waste disposal

Keywords

Radionuclides Speciation Sorption Nuclear Waste

Biogeochemistry

Interactions between microorganisms and geochemical processes

  • Geomicrobial impacts on trace elements and radionuclides
  • Microbial sequestration of arsenic in soil-water systems
  • Biogeochemical cycling at the microbe–mineral interface

Keywords

Geomicrobiology Biogeochemistry Redox
